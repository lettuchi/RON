# 嘘の街の赤い糸 — *Uso no Machi no Akai Ito*
### "The Red String in the City of Lies" — Main Menu Opening Song

- **Game:** Ryoko Owari Nights
- **Use:** Title / main-menu theme (`game/audio/bgm/main_menu_song.mp3`)
- **Style:** 2000s-era anime opening, Japanese vocal
- **Vocal:** female, warm/grounded/feminine (Toa's POV — not high-pitched "ani-goe")
- **Themes:** the city of lies, the red string of fate (赤い糸), red saturating the city, accepting him even his cruelty, devotion with a boundary, love that is real whether or not it's returned

> **Key chorus line:** 返されなくても、この愛は本物 — *Kaesarenakutemo, kono ai wa honmono* — "Love doesn't need to be returned to be real."
