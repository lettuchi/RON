# 「赤い糸の灯」 (Akai Ito no Hi / *Lantern of the Red Thread*)



**Toa image song** — Kakita Toa, Crane dancer, Ryoko Owari.



Line-by-line alignment across Japanese (as sung), romaji, and English.



---



## Intro (soft)



| Japanese | Romaji | English |

|----------|--------|---------|

| 運河の灯　ひとつ　またひとつ | Unga no hi, hitotsu, mata hitotsu | Canal lanterns — one, then another |

| 赤い糸　指先に　巻きかけて | Akai ito, yubisaki ni, makikakete | I wind the red thread around my fingertips |



---



## Verse 1



| Japanese | Romaji | English |

|----------|--------|---------|

| 嘘の街　水面は鏡 | Uso no machi, minamo wa kagami | City of lies — the water is a mirror |

| 誰の顔も　本当は隠れる | Dare no kao mo, hontō wa kakureru | Every face hides the truth |

| 黒い袖　帯の鶴が泳ぐ | Kuroi sode, obi no tsuru ga oyogu | Black sleeves — the crane on my obi swims |

| 踊りは終わった　と思っていた | Odori wa owatta, to omotte ita | I thought the dance was over |

| あなたの笑い　冷たい扉 | Anata no warai, tsumetai tobira | Your smile, a cold door |

| それでも　指は離さない | Soredemo, yubi wa hanasanai | Still my fingers won't let go |

| 赤い糸　見えなくても | Akai ito, mienakute mo | The red thread — even unseen |

| 確かに　胸の奥で結んでいる | Tashika ni, mune no oku de musunde iru | Surely tied deep in my chest |

## Chorus



| Japanese | Romaji | English |

|----------|--------|---------|

| まだ結ぶ　この糸を | Mada musubu, kono ito o | I still tie this thread |

| 嘘つきの夜に　灯をともす | Usotsuki no yoru ni, hi o tomosu | I light a lamp on lying nights |

| 閉ざす扉　残酷な言葉 | Tozasu tobira, zankoku na kotoba | Closed doors, cruel words |

| わたしは知って　それでも戻る | Watashi wa shitte, soredemo modoru | I know you — and I still return |

| 鶴の帯が　風に揺れて | Tsuru no obi ga, kaze ni yurete | The crane obi sways in the wind |

| 運河を見下ろす　あなたの背中 | Unga o miorosu, anata no senaka | Your back overlooking the canal |

| 遠いままで　いいから | Tōi mama de, ii kara | Far is fine |

| ここに　いたい | Koko ni, itai | I want to be here |

## Verse 2



| Japanese | Romaji | English |

|----------|--------|---------|

| 雨の石畳　傘の下で | Ame no ishidatami, kasa no shita de | Rain on cobblestones, under the umbrella |

| 甘いものを　二人で分けた | Amai mono o, futari de waketa | We shared something sweet, just us two |

| 「道具」なんて　言わないでほしい | "Dōgu" nante, iwanaide hoshii | Don't call me a tool |

| でも言われても　涙は隠さない | Demo iwarete mo, namida wa kakusanai | Even if you do, I won't hide my tears |

| 帳の重さ　背負う人は | Chō no omosa, seou hito wa | The one who carries the ledger's weight |

| 壊れやすい　硝子みたい | Kowareyasui, garasu mitai | Is fragile as glass |

| わたしの嘘は　一つだけ | Watashi no uso wa, hitotsu dake | My only lie is one |

| 「平気」——　本当は平気じゃない | "Heiki"—— hontō wa heiki ja nai | "I'm fine" — I'm not fine |

## Bridge (gentle lift)



| Japanese | Romaji | English |

|----------|--------|---------|

| 切れても　また結び直す | Kirete mo, mata musubinaosu | Even if it snaps, I tie it again |

| 運命じゃなく　選んだから | Unmei ja naku, eranda kara | Not fate — I chose this |

| この街が　全部嘘でも | Kono machi ga, zenbu uso demo | Even if the whole city lies |

| わたしの想いだけ　本当にしたい | Watashi no omoi dake, hontō ni shitai | I want my feeling alone to be true |

| あなたの名を　呼ばなくても | Anata no na o, yobanakute mo | Without calling your name |

| わたしは　ここにいる | Watashi wa, koko ni iru | I'm still here |

## Final chorus



| Japanese | Romaji | English |

|----------|--------|---------|

| まだ結ぶ　赤い糸を | Mada musubu, akai ito o | I still tie the red thread |

| 嘘の朝に　希望の色 | Uso no asa ni, kibō no iro | On a lying dawn, the color of hope |

| 閉ざす扉　開かなくても | Tozasu tobira, akanakute mo | Doors stay shut |

| わたしの灯は　消えない | Watashi no hi wa, kienai | My lantern won't go out |

| 鶴の帯が　夜明けに光る | Tsuru no obi ga, yoake ni hikaru | Crane obi gleams at daybreak |

| 運河の向こう　まだ見えない未来 | Unga no mukō, mada mienai mirai | A future beyond the canal, not yet seen |

| 遠いままで　いいから | Tōi mama de, ii kara | Far is fine |

| ずっと　結んでいる | Zutto, musunde iru | Still tied |
