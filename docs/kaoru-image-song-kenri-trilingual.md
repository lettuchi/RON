# 「権利の帳」 (Kenri no Chō / *The Ledger of Rights*)

**Kaoru image song** — Kitsu Kaoru, Emerald Magistrate, Ryoko Owari.

Line-by-line alignment across Japanese (as sung), romaji, and English.

---

## Intro (spoken)

| Japanese | Romaji | English |
|----------|--------|---------|
| （帳をパラパラとめくる音） | *(chō o parapara to mekuru oto)* | *(sound of pages flipping through a ledger)* |
| 「記録は嘘を裁かない。裁くのは、私だ。」 | "Kiroku wa uso o sabakanai. Sabaku no wa, watashi da." | "The record doesn't judge lies. *I* do." |
