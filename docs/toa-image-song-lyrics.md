# Toa image song — 「赤い糸の灯」 (*Akai Ito no Hi* / *Lantern of the Red Thread*)

**Character:** Kakita Toa — Crane dancer (player-facing: dancer, not yoriki). White hair and eyelashes, grey eyes, black kosode, gold crane obi, white hakama.

**Tone vs Kaoru「権利の帳」:** Softer, brighter, hopeful Hakuoki-style **heroine image song** — clear female vocal, gentle pop/orchestral (~118–126 BPM), not raspy or aggressive. *I know what you are, but I stay.*

## English sense-translation (subtitles / music-gen prompt)

- **Intro:** Canal lanterns — one, then another; I wind the red thread around my fingertips.
- **V1:** City of lies — the water is a mirror; every face hides the truth. Black sleeves, the crane on my obi swims; I thought the dance was over. Your smile, a cold door — still my fingers won't let go. The red thread, even unseen, is surely tied deep in my chest.
- **Pre:** I know it isn't kindness — still I want it.
- **Chorus:** I still tie this thread; I light a lamp on lying nights. Closed doors, cruel words — I know you, and I still return. The crane obi sways; your back overlooking the canal. Far is fine — I want to be here.
- **V2:** Rain on cobblestones, under one umbrella we shared something sweet. Don't call me a tool — even if you do, I won't hide my tears. The one who carries the ledger is fragile as glass. My only lie is "I'm fine" — I'm not fine.
- **Bridge:** Even if it snaps, I tie it again — not fate, I chose this. Even if the whole city lies, I want my feeling alone to be true. Without calling your name — I'm still here.
- **Final:** I still tie the red thread; on a lying dawn, the color of hope. Doors stay shut — my lantern won't go out. Crane obi at daybreak; a future beyond the canal. Far is fine — still tied.
- **Outro:** Another canal lantern — still, I tie.
