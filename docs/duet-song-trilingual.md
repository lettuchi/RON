# 「嘘の夜を走れ」 (Uso no Yoru o Hashire / *Run Through the Lying Night*)



**Toa + Kaoru duet** — high-energy anime duet, all sung.



---



## Intro (sung)



| Japanese | Romaji | English |

|----------|--------|---------|

| 雨音が　鼓膜を叩く | Ameoto ga, komaku o tataku | Rain hammers the eardrums |

| 嘘の街で　二人　走り出す | Uso no machi de, futari hashiridasu | In the city of lies, the two of us break into a run |



---



## Verse 1 — Toa (sung)



| Japanese | Romaji | English |

|----------|--------|---------|

| 黒い袖　風に鳴る | Kuroi sode, kaze ni naru | Black sleeves sing in the wind |

| 白い鶴は　夜を刺す | Shiroi tsuru wa, yoru o sasu | The white crane pierces the night |

| 冷たさを　知ってるのに | Tsumetasa o, shitteru noni | I know your coldness |

| この視線は　まだ熱い | Kono shisen wa, mada atsui | Still this gaze burns hot |

## Verse 1 — Kaoru (sung)
