# Kaoru dialogue style (Kitsu Kaoru, Emerald Magistrate)

Use this when writing or revising Kaoru lines in Ryoko Owari. Counterpart: [toa-dialogue-style.md](toa-dialogue-style.md). Voice refs: [discord-threads-raw.md](discord-threads-raw.md), [voice-design-prompts.md](voice-design-prompts.md).

## Core voice

- **Dry Emerald Magistrate:** short imperatives, concrete props (desk, seal, hanko, docket, wax, corridor, cushion on tatami, archive stairs, ledger line).
- **Teasing dominance, proprietary warmth:** *To-chan*, sponsorship-not-danna, *I know what I am paying for*, amused cruelty — not zen distance.
- **Discord anchors:** wrong-door energy, paperwork as leverage, permit dates moved forward, curry shop as insult, companion = witness + schedule.

## Avoid

- Fortune-cookie / koan lines: predicate comparisons (*X is Y; Z is W*), *silence hides a name*, *survival is currency*, *docket is a blade not a story*.
- Vague haunting poetry when a bureaucratic cut works (*half-haunted city*, *writing poetry with corpses*).
- Generic mentor aphorisms; prefer what he will **sign**, **seal**, or **deny**.

## Good patterns

- Command + object: *Desk. Now.* / *Bag it for custody.*
- Ledger / watch / tax logic: *The watch files drowning when the ledger line stays blank.*
- Proprietary nicknames sparingly: *Keep up, To-chan.*
- Sponsorship frame: art donor to  at first; never danna; *I know what I am paying for.* by Case 3 the sponsorship element is mostly forgotten. Toa is defacto live-in lover by case 5.

## Bad → good (from Case 1 pass, 2026-06-03)

| Bad | Good |
|-----|------|
| Gratitude is cheap. Obedience is currency. Spend it on the archive stairs. | Thank me with a dry ledger, not your mouth. Archive stairs. Now. |
| Ryoko Owari buries its dead without funerals when the ledger prefers silence. You will learn the city's arithmetic. | The watch files drowning when the ledger line stays blank. You will learn which boxes they sign. |
| Finally — someone who understands a docket is a blade, not a story. | Good. Witness notes I can paste without crossing out your adjectives. |
| This city is always half-haunted. Most nights we blame the living. | Wrong-colored water usually means a clerk poured something upstream. Check the eaves before you blame charms. |
| Scorpion comb in Crane water. Someone is writing poetry with corpses. | Scorpion comb in Crane water. Bag it for custody — do not compose metaphors on my desk. |
| Fear is the only currency this quarter mints honestly. I'll spend it. | She will talk while she is frightened. Ask before the okami sells her courage back. |
| In this city the difference is who holds the key. Tonight, that is me. | Escort means my corridor, my hanko, my release. Tonight you do not leave without all three. |

## Checklist before shipping a Kaoru line

1. Can you point at a **prop or form** (seal, line, stairs, cup)?
2. Would he say it to a clerk, not a monk?
3. Is warmth **proprietary** (To-chan, my hall, my desk) rather than mystical?
4. Cut one abstract noun if the line still works.
