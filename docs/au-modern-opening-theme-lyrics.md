# Modern AU Opening Theme — Lyrics

**Title:** 「申請中の恋」 *Shinsei-chū no Koi* ("Love, Pending Approval")

**Style:** Anime OP, ~90 seconds. Bright J-pop with a rainy bureau-noir undertone. Suggested: piano + clipped strings intro, four-on-the-floor pop chorus, harbor-rain ambience. Toa POV, with an optional Kaoru duet bridge.

**Use:** Pairs with the Wrong Floor opening establishing shot (`au_modern_tower_establishing`) / a Modern AU OP sequence.

> Motifs woven in: wrong conference room, the almost-smile hidden behind a stamp, "Deputy Director" (副所長), three business days, "not a date on letterhead," clementines (みかん), witness posture, "motion denied," and "effective immediately."

---

## [Intro]
> 濡れた傘、十四階B。押したドアは、ひとつ隣。
> *Nureta kasa, jūyon-kai B. Oshita tobira wa, hitotsu tonari.*
> (A wet umbrella, floor fourteen B. The door I pushed was one over.)

## [Verse 1]
押したドアは ひとつ隣の朝
「また会議室、間違い」 あなたが笑う
判子の影に 微笑みを隠して
副所長の声が 私を呼び止める

*Oshita doa wa hitotsu tonari no asa*
*"Mata kaigishitsu, machigai" anata ga warau*
*Hanko no kage ni hohoemi o kakushite*
*Fukushochō no koe ga watashi o yobitomeru*

> The door I pushed opened on the morning next door.
> "Wrong conference room. Again," you almost laugh.
> You hide the smile in the shadow of your stamp.
> The Deputy Director's voice calls me back.

## [Pre-Chorus]
三営業日 待てないこの鼓動
書類の余白に 本音がにじむ

*San eigyōbi matenai kono kodō*
*Shorui no yohaku ni honne ga nijimu*

> Three business days — this heartbeat can't wait that long.
> My real feelings bleed into the margins of the forms.

## [Chorus]
許可して この恋を
レターヘッドに のらなくても
港の雨 ネオン揺れて
「不許可」と言いながら 帰さないで
申請中 まだ 申請中

*Kyoka shite kono koi o*
*Retāheddo ni noranakute mo*
*Minato no ame, neon yurete*
*"Fukyoka" to iinagara kaesanaide*
*Shinsei-chū, mada shinsei-chū*

> Approve this love,
> even if it never goes on letterhead.
> Harbor rain, the neon swaying —
> you say "denied," but you won't send me home.
> Pending. Still pending.

## [Verse 2]
みかんの皮 規則を少し曲げて
証人席で 見ていたのは あなた
傘の下 半歩の距離のまま
押せない判子 ひとつ 胸の奥

*Mikan no kawa, kisoku o sukoshi magete*
*Shōninseki de miteita no wa anata*
*Kasa no shita, hanpo no kyori no mama*
*Osenai hanko hitotsu, mune no oku*

> Peeling a clementine, bending the rule just a little.
> From the witness seat, the one I was watching was you.
> Under the umbrella, still half a step apart —
> one stamp I can't press, deep in my chest.

## [Bridge] *(optional duet)*
（Kaoru）「動議は却下」 そう言って
（Toa） 笑いかけて、止めないで
（Both） 効力は たぶん 今すぐ

*(Kaoru) "Dōgi wa kyakka" sō itte*
*(Toa) Waraikakete, tomenaide*
*(Both) Kōryoku wa, tabun, ima sugu*

> (Kaoru) "Motion denied," you say —
> (Toa) you start to smile, so don't stop.
> (Both) Effective, maybe, immediately.

## [Last Chorus]
受理して この気持ちを
日付も 名前も 書き込んで
雨が止んでも 消えない判
「却下」のはずが まだ ここにいる
申請中 ずっと 申請中

*Juri shite kono kimochi o*
*Hizuke mo namae mo kakikonde*
*Ame ga yandemo kienai han*
*"Kyakka" no hazu ga mada koko ni iru*
*Shinsei-chū, zutto shinsei-chū*

> Accept this feeling on the record.
> Write in the date, write in the name.
> A seal that won't fade even after the rain stops.
> It should have been denied, yet here I still am.
> Pending. Always pending.

### Suno duet note (if scoring as a Toa/Kaoru duet)
Reinforce in three places: **style** ("duet, Toa female / Kaoru male, J-pop anime OP, harbor rain"), a **header** at the top of the lyrics (`[duet: Toa female, Kaoru male]`), and **per-line labels** (`[Toa]`, `[Kaoru]`, `[Both]`) — keep the bridge as the only `[Both]` overlap for stability.
