# Duet — 「嘘の夜を走れ」 (*Uso no Yoru o Hashire* / *Run Through the Lying Night*)

**Characters:** Kakita Toa + Kitsu Kaoru, Ryoko Owari.

**Tone:** High-energy **anime duet** (OP / image-song) — **all sung**, no spoken dialogue. Female soprano + male baritone with **harmonized choruses**. Forward motion, devotion, tension — not musical theatre.

**Audio:** `game/audio/bgm/duet_song.mp3`

**Legend:** `[sung]` solo or call-response · `[harmony]` both voices together

**Avoid:** ledger (帳), red string (赤い糸), spoken dialogue. OK: canals, lanterns, rain, lies, running, two lights, closed doors.

## English sense-translation (montage subtitles)

- **Intro:** Rain hammers the eardrums — in the city of lies, the two of us break into a run.
- **Toa V1:** Black sleeves sing in the wind; the white crane pierces the night. I know your coldness — still this gaze burns hot.
- **Kaoru V1:** A laughing night beyond the rain; distance itself is sweet poison. I won't return you, won't let you go — that's the rule for us two.
- **Pre:** This heartbeat won't stop; this night isn't over yet.
- **Chorus:** Run through the lying night, together — canal lanterns blaze. Can't grasp you, won't release you — we sing across the waves. Cruel as it is, morning comes; in the city of lies, a true voice. Run through the lying night.
- **Toa V2:** After tears, go on with a smile; reach past your shadow. I don't want victory — I want the heartbeat beside me. I want to keep running.
- **Kaoru V2:** The night lines up lies; your silence becomes the beat. A sweet voice can be a blade too — you're still here.
- **Bridge:** Doors stay shut — our two lights won't die. Without calling your name, I know — still here.
- **Final:** Same run; lying dawn, color of hope; Kakita Toa, Kitsu Kaoru — still here.
- **Outro:** The night remains — run, together.
