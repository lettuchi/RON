## One-line premise

Kaoru books a **private, venue-buyout dinner** on Tear Drop Island to show appreciation for Toa's work; Toa arrives in formal spring furisode, they share kaiseki and a dance performance, she teaches him to read bodies like a magistrate reads lies — then **city gossip** about whether she is safe with him surfaces, he reassures her she is loved, and players confirm OOC: **it was a date** (never asked in-character in the thread).

## Plot summary (beat by beat)

### 1. Invitation — what is this?

- Kaoru sends precise instructions: **Tear Drop Island**, inn between two geisha houses (north side), **wear your best**.
- Toa agonizes over hairpins, obi, shoes; chooses black spring furisode (cherry/grape hyacinth, rose-gold obi, zori, rouge) and travels slowly in formal dress.

### 2. Arrival — private venue

- Toa learns Kaoru **bought out the whole place**; no other guests. Hojicha; shock and delight.
- Staff confirm **performances on request** from neighboring geisha houses.
- Toa greets him formally (*Good evening, Magistrate-sama*); stiff obi makes bowing comic; she notices his fragrance (callback to first meeting).

### 3. Dinner — appreciation without “thank you”

- Kaoru: hungry/thirsty? Menu already set — kaiseki reflecting season transition (asparagus/plum blossom, skipjack, fiddlehead miso soup, toro/uni porridge, bamboo/shrimp, mushroom shabushabu, etc.).
- He frames the evening as showing **appreciation for the work you have done for me** — not the word *thank you* (Toa notes he has thanked her before in his own way).
- Sake poured; biwa music; optional more music/dancers.
- Toa: first music/dance **for fun** in a long time — *Kampai!* They eat in companionable focus (*itadakimasu*, minimal chatter).

### 4. Performance — dancer’s eye

**CG outfit lock (VN):** Toa — black spring **furisode**, cherry/grape-hyacinth motifs, **rose-gold obi**; Kaoru — **gold/mustard ochre magistrate haori** (same arc as festival kiss / duo-table). They are **audience**, not performers — `cg-case3_5-date-watch-dancers.png` at the dancer beat (`case3_5_date_watch_dancers`).

- Full ensemble (strings, flute, hand drum); geisha dancers bow and perform.
- Kaoru: *If I ever find dance crimes I know who to bring* — asks her to relax, enjoy, or critique.
- Toa lecture (core beat): performance is practice; read **mindset in movement**, not mistakes; pretense stumbles; body is truthteller; parallels **magistrate reading deceit**.
- She demos expertise: reads each dancer (substitute in back, nervous junior up front, one making eyes at patron vs dancers who dance to dance vs body-as-message).

### 5. Trust — why she believes in him

- Kaoru connects her skill to general investigation.
- Toa overshares: people weekly ask if she is okay, if he is **hurting** her, if she is with him freely; she trusts him because **bodies don’t lie** — and that is why she has always trusted **you**. Almost confesses she loves him but stops short.
- Kaoru laughs, cuts off the vulnerable tail: **I know.** Then insists: who said this? (**I insist.**)

### 6. Gossip — names and face-saving

- Toa flustered (adrenaline, stutter): e.g. **Hihihiromi-san**, **Doji Sango-san** (more NPCs planned).
- Kaoru amused especially by Sango; delights in rumor-network as intelligence; tells Toa not to change what she tells people.
- Toa spirals: do you hear bad things about **me**?

### 7. Reassurance — loved in the city

- Kaoru: **Never once. You are very well loved in this city. ((Maybe as close as Kaoru could get to saying "I love you, too."**
- Toa beams; tension drops.
- **Dessert?** — *Of course! What's a fancy meal without it?*

### 8. OOC coda — the title question

- Toa OOC: `(( scene? btw it's totally a date ))`
- Kaoru OOC: unsure where to take scene; later agrees scene can end here (no IC sex continuation in thread).

## Tone and attachment arc (Case 3.5)

- **Not** a love confession scene. Discord ends with **reassurance and dessert**, not *I love you* or *this was a date* in IC dialogue.
- **Attachment level:** post–Case 3 **devotion + physical awareness** (smell, rouge, furisode, *very well loved*). Fits `toa-dialogue-style.md` Case 3–4 band: hunger and trust, romance vocabulary still unspoken IC.
- **Kaoru:** proprietary hospitality (buyout, curated menu, *I know*) without naming courtship; gossip delight is classic Emerald Magistrate play.
- **Humor:** stiff obi bow, coin-flip shoes, uni porridge OOC, Kaoru’s *dance crimes* line.
- **Pain point:** outsiders assume Toa is coerced; she defends him in public; he cares who is watching **him**, she cares if anyone maligns **her**.
