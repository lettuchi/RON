# Toa dialogue style (Kakita Toa, Crane dancer)

Use this when writing or revising Toa lines in Ryoko Owari. Pair with [kaoru-dialogue-style.md](kaoru-dialogue-style.md). Canon spine: [story-so-far.md](story-so-far.md). Voice refs: [discord-threads-raw.md](discord-threads-raw.md), [voice-design-prompts.md](voice-design-prompts.md).

## Who she is

- **Kakita Toa:** post-gempukku Crane dancer, white hair, grey eyes, black kosode, gold crane obi, white hakama — honest, cheerful, direct (see vn-art-pipeline Toa spec).
- **Arrives** with an expired permit, wrong-door energy, no local patrons — needs Ryoko Owari to become *home*, not just a posting.
- **Role in cases:** live-in companion / art patronage witness (not yoriki); learns magistrate work through props (ledger, comb, seal petals, witness notes), snacks as comfort rhythm.

## Voice / register

- **Bright Crane-trained adult:** early-twenties, warm medium alto, musical lift on questions — spirited, not childish, not dominatrix (that is Kaoru).
- **Deferential without shrinking:** *Magistrate-sama* with sincere devotion and light sparkle; *sochira-no-kata* / formal petition register when frightened or at thresholds.
- **Concrete over poetry:** permit book, obijime, third bell, curry shop, pony shoes, archive dust — she notices bodies and paperwork, not abstract fate metaphors.
- **Humor:** self-deprecating geography jokes, snack-after-paperwork running gag, stubborn one-liners when pride flares.

## Relationship arc (what she feels vs what she says)

| Phase | Attachment | She *feels* / shows | She will **not** say yet |
|-------|------------|---------------------|---------------------------|
| **Prologue** | Survival + wary pride | Lost corridors, renewal panic, dance-for-ink bargain; flustered respect after canon scene | Love, destiny, “I chose you forever” |
| **Case 1–2** | Trust + professional hunger | *Magistrate-sama*, escort terms, room adjoining yours, shock at stipend; intimacy as negotiated duty / sponsorship | “I love you”; comparing him to Academy suitors; planning a life beyond the hall |
| **Case 3** | **Fast attachment** (devotion, reliance, approval-seeking) | Wants his nod on notes; hurt if he dismisses her witness work; stays when scared; sponsorship label fades in daily speech | Naming romantic love; jealousy framed as romance; “leave the city with me” |
| **Case 4** | Deep habit, fear of loss | Jealousy of his time, body-memory intimacy, protectiveness in investigation; may say *yours* in heat — still thinks it is arrangement + hunger | Clear-eyed “I am in love with you” |
| **Case 5~** | **Slow love realization** | Finally names love (or admits she cannot leave) after plot pressure; red-string / future talk earned | — |

**Rule of thumb:** Through Case 3 she calls it **duty, patronage, Magistrate-sama, the hall, the room** — not **heart**. Fluster and physical want are allowed; romantic self-awareness waits.

## With Kaoru vs alone vs others

| Context | Register |
|---------|----------|
| **With Kaoru** | *Magistrate-sama* (canon intimacy); playful pushback on terms; witness-note diction when working; soft beats after danger — thanks for room, escort, seal — without love declarations (pre–Case 5). |
| **Alone / narrator-adjacent** | Snack-order jokes, pride inventory (*wet book and a very good bow*), Crane spine (*Mother taught me the spine first*), permits and hunger — still concrete. |
| **Others (Suzu, clerks, watch)** | Polite Crane performer; geisha-house empathy (*only funeral he's going to get*); firmer when lied to; never modern slang or fourth-wall. |

## Avoid

- Fourth wall, player address, meta VN jokes.
- Koan-speak / fortune-cookie parallels (*fear is currency*, *silence hides a name*) — leave that off Kaoru’s corrected lines too.
- Modern slang (*oh fuck yes*, *gimme a minute*, *how 'bout that*) in shipped Ren'Py — Discord NSFW tone stays in adaptation notes, not default investigation voice.
- Childish squeal, loli cadence, constant *uwu* — adult dancer.
- Generic shōjo confession before Case 5 arc beat.
- Competing with Kaoru’s dry bureaucracy — she **reports**; he **rules**.

## Checklist before shipping a Toa line

1. **Prop or stake?** (permit, ledger line, comb, tea, room, bell)
2. **Arc-correct attachment?** (Case 3 = devotion, not love confession)
3. **Adult Crane register?** (trained diction, not teen sitcom)
4. **Magistrate-sama** earned in scene — not every line, not sarcastic
5. Cut one abstract noun if the line still lands
